# UVP and positioning

## Position

For someone deciding dinner from a fridge they only half remember, FreshLoop is the inventory-first assistant that starts from dates they typed and recipes that fit. It shows what the pantry covers, what it does not, and what it refuses to guess. It is not a safety checker and not a shop that buys the food.

## UVP

Know what to cook before your groceries go to waste.

The line is a promise about the decision, not a promise that waste will fall by a measured amount. No pilot has been run.

## Reasons to believe, inside the product

These are design commitments, not outcome claims.

| Commitment | Where it shows |
| --- | --- |
| Your dates, not our verdict | Use soon, Date passed, Opened, and No date. Each has a sentence. |
| Your quantities, or no subtraction | Have, Short, and Check on the recipe. Check stays on the shopping list. |
| One pantry, counted once | The four-day plan builds one list. |
| Staples are an assumption you can see | Salt, pepper, and water. The switch is in Settings. |
| Nothing is saved from a scan until you say so | Barcode result banner. Receipt scan says it is not connected. |
| The list still works if you refuse reminders | Permission denied state. |

## What we will not say

- “This food is safe.”
- “Throw this out.”
- “Allergen-free” or “certified.”
- “You wasted less this week,” until a real baseline exists.
- “3.29% waste” or any other company’s statistic as if it were ours.

## Audience language

Plain kitchen language. Name the food, the amount, and the date type. Avoid guilt (“you let this spoil”) and avoid cheer (“rescue this ingredient”). The tone is a careful note on the fridge, not a campaign.

## Category

Closest category: food inventory with recipe match. The position inside that category is the transparent gap, not the largest catalog and not the fastest scanner.

## Message for a portfolio review

FreshLoop tests whether an inventory-first workflow can make the cook-or-shop decision clearer without borrowing authority the data does not have.
