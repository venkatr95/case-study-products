## Responsive rule on every frame

One column under 960px. Side navigation from 960px. Bottom navigation on small screens: Pantry, Cook, Shop, Plan. Targets at least 44px. The footer line of each SVG states that rule.

## Reading order

A reviewer should read WF-01, WF-03, WF-06, WF-08, WF-09, then WF-11. That is the UVP. WF-02 and WF-05 show the first-run and the duplicate. WF-10 and WF-12 show the shop and the combined plan. WF-13 through WF-16 are the trust states.
