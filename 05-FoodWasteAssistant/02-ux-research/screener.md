# Screener

Use this before booking. It filters for recent cooking and shopping. It does not ask people to rate food waste or to want an app.

**Status:** Not fielded. No respondents.

## Invite line

We are interviewing people about how they decide what to cook and what to buy. 45 minutes, paid, remote. You do not need to use any particular app.

## Questions

Answer rules are for the recruiter, not for the participant to see.

**S1. In the last month, how many days did you cook a meal at home?**

- 0 — stop
- 1–2 — stop for the primary round; hold only if secondary quota is open and S3 is “I shop for myself”
- 3 or more — continue

**S2. In the last month, did you buy groceries for a household (more than yourself)?**

- Yes — primary candidate
- No, I shop for myself — secondary candidate
- No, someone else shops and I do not — stop, unless they are the person who decides meals and they shop at least twice a month. If unclear, stop.

**S3. Which best describes your kitchen right now?**

- I share cooking or shopping with other people in my home — note “shared,” do not exclude
- I cook and shop for myself — secondary
- I rarely use the kitchen — stop

**S4. Do you currently keep any list of food you already have at home?**

- Yes, and I updated it in the last 14 days
- I used to, and I stopped
- No

Recruit at least two “stopped” and at least two “no.” A full set of current power-users would hide the maintenance problem.

**S5. In the last month, have you used any of these?**

Select all. Record names. Do not exclude on this question. It is context.

- A recipe website or app
- A notes app or paper list for groceries
- A food inventory or pantry app
- None of these

**S6. Are you 18 or older?**

- No — stop
- Yes — continue

**S7. Is planning meals or testing kitchen software your paid job?**

- Yes — stop for this round
- No — continue

## Quotas for six seats

| Seat | Rule |
| --- | --- |
| 4 | Primary: cooks 3+ days, shops for a household |
| 2 | Secondary: cooks 3+ days, shops for themselves |
| At least 2 of the 6 | Stopped keeping a food list, or never kept one |
| At least 1 of the 6 | Currently keeps a list |

Do not add a quota for dietary pattern. Ask about it in the interview if they raise it.

## What not to ask in the screener

- “Do you hate wasting food?”
- “Would you pay for an inventory app?”
- Income, health conditions, or household address.
