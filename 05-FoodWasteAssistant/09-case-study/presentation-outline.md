# Presentation outline

Eight slides. Proposed concept. Say “validation planned” on the first slide and do not skip it.

## 1. Title

FreshLoop. Know what to cook before your groceries go to waste. Role, proposed timeline, prototype status. One line: no user testing yet.

Visual: welcome screenshot.

## 2. The decision, not the catalog

Households forget food and buy it again. Recipe tools start from the dish. The bet is to start from dates the person entered. Label the bet as a hypothesis.

Visual: journey diagram from `assets/diagrams/core-flow.md`.

## 3. What we refused to build

No safety score. No shelf-life table. No silent unit conversion. No fake receipt scan. No shared kitchen until conflict rules exist. One sentence on why each refusal protects the UVP.

Visual: none, or the recovery and reminder wireframes.

## 4. Competitive read

NoWaste, Samsung Food, SuperCook, FoodKeeper. They already do pieces of inventory and lists. The gap we designed for is visible arithmetic. Cite the date of the review. Do not show a feature matrix that marks competitors as missing barcodes.

Visual: one comparison table, five rows, from the case study.

## 5. The loop

Pantry count, Use soon, recipe match, review, shop or cooked. Call out the sample: spinach covered, yogurt Check, two dinners producing one lemon and one garlic.

Visual: pantry screenshot, then recipe screenshot.

## 6. Wireframe to interface

Show WF-09 review beside the high-fidelity review. The point is the two groups: to buy, and left off.

Visual: `wf-09-review-list.svg` and a shop or review capture.

## 7. Trust and access

Date words. Diet limitation. Opt-in reminders that end when the page closes. Export and delete. Contrast checked in tests. Screen reader not yet run.

Visual: settings screenshot.

## 8. What would change our mind

If interviews say people will not log food, stop adding recipes. If usability participants think a date means “safe,” rewrite the copy before any provider work. Targets: 75% and 70%, unmeasured.

Visual: empty findings template, shown as empty.

## Speaker notes to avoid

- “Users loved.”
- “We reduced waste by.”
- “Competitors don’t have meal plans.”
- “Figma prototype” — there is not one.
