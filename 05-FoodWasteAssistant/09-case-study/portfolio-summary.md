# Portfolio summary

## Card

**FreshLoop**  
Know what to cook before your groceries go to waste.

A mobile-first concept for cooking from food already at home. The pantry leads with dates the person entered. Recipes show what is covered, what is short, and what cannot be compared. The shopping list subtracts a quantity only when the units match.

Role: product design, UX writing, design system, TypeScript prototype.  
Status: validation planned. No interviews or usability sessions yet.

## One paragraph

FreshLoop is an inventory-first kitchen, not a recipe feed and not a food-safety tool. I specified the match rules, the date language, and the states for duplicate items, denied reminders, missing scanners, and unreadable saved data. The prototype is Vite and TypeScript. Sixteen annotated wireframe SVGs stand in for Figma, which was not used. Success targets from the brief are written as a test plan, not as results.

## Captions

**Welcome.** The promise is the title. The next sentence says dates are reminders, not a safety decision. Sample data is not on this screen.

**Pantry.** Fictional sample kitchen. The first number is how many items fall inside the person’s window. Date passed is a separate sentence. Statuses are words.

**Use soon.** Opened is listed and is not treated as an expiry. Caption should say the dates were entered for the demo, relative to the day the screenshot was taken.

**Recipe.** Original sample recipe. Have, Short, Check, Missing, Staple, and Optional are labels. This is not a licensed recipe page.

**Shop, empty.** The list before any review is committed. It points back to Cook and does not invent lines. The gap arithmetic is on the recipe and in the review step, which the automated flow covers. A static screenshot cannot commit the review by itself.

**Wireframe.** Grayscale, 390×844 inside a 1040×980 board, annotations on the right. Not a Figma file.

**Desktop pantry.** Same kitchen at 960px and wider, with side navigation.
