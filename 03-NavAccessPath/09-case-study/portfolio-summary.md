# Portfolio card

**AccessPath** — Find routes that work for your mobility needs, not just the shortest route.

Mobile-first concept for comparing a walk by stairs, curb ramps, and steepness, with confirmed, community-reported, and unknown kept as different facts. Working TypeScript prototype. No user study yet. Seattle data in the demo is illustrative.

**Show:** preference setup, the three-route comparison, the south arcade entrance, the pending report.
**Do not claim:** a measured success rate, a partnership with AccessMap or the City of Seattle, or that the routes are safe to walk.

## Suggested captions

- **Preferences.** "Step-free is a set of settings, not a label for a person."
- **Comparison.** "The shortest route conflicts. The matching route is longer, and it says so."
- **Unknown route.** "Missing grade and a missing curb ramp block a match. They are not treated as fine."
- **Entrance.** "Two community reports stay on the page. The door is still unknown."
- **Report.** "Pending review means it has not been checked."
- **Wireframe.** "Status order was set in grayscale, before the green."
