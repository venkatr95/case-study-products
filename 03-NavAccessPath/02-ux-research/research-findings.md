# Research findings

**Status:** No study has been run. This document is the synthesis template plus the design hypotheses the prototype currently assumes. Hypotheses are not findings.

## How to file a real note

Copy the block once per interview. Do not invent quotes to fill it.

```text
Participant code:
Date:
Question number:
Evidence (what they did or said):
Quote:
Source note (recording time or note line):
Interpretation:
Design implication:
Disconfirms an existing hypothesis? yes / no
```

## Findings

None. The quote column is empty on purpose.

## Hypotheses the design is using

These are restated so a later finding can knock them down. They are not participant data.

| ID | Hypothesis | Where it shows up | What would retire it |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| H1 | People get partway down a "short" route before they learn about stairs. | Pine Street direct stays on the comparison, labeled as a conflict. | Interviews where people already filter stairs successfully and do not want the failing route. |
| H2 | Silence about an attribute is misread as "fine." | Unknown is a chip and blocks a match when the setting is on. | People who already treat blanks as unknown without help. |
| H3 | A single wheelchair mode does not cover a stroller day or a low-grade day. | Three presets with different settings. | People who say one step-free switch is the only control they will use. |
| H4 | Crowd notes are useful only with a visible review state. | Pending versus verified. Community text cannot confirm an attribute. | People who either ignore all crowd notes or trust them as much as a city survey. |
| H5 | Conflicting notes should both remain visible. | South arcade entrance lists a step report and a ramp report. | People who need the product to pick one and will abandon a list. |

## Interpretation rules for later

- A quote supports a finding only with a source note.
- One interview can create a hypothesis. It cannot create a percentage.
- Do not backfill this file with prototype feedback from the author. Author review belongs in `07-prototype/prototype-review.md`.
