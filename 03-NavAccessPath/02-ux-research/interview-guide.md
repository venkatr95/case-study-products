# Interview guide

**Use:** Discovery interviews. Do not show AccessPath until the past-trip section is finished.
**Time:** 45 minutes.
**Tone:** Ask about the last real trip. Do not ask people to imagine that they love the concept.

## Intro (3 minutes)

Thank them. Explain that the session is about how they planned a recent trip, that there are no right answers, and that they can skip any question. Read the consent. Start the recording only if they agreed.

Say: "I'll ask about a trip you actually took. I won't ask for a diagnosis."

## Recent trip (15 minutes)

1. Think of the last time you planned a walk, or a transit trip with a walk, in a place you did not already know well. What was the trip?
2. What did you need to be true about the route that day? Ask only if they pause: stairs, hills, curbs, elevators, a stroller, bags, walking with someone else, time.
3. What did you look up before you left? Where did you look, and in what order?
4. What did each source tell you? What did it not tell you?
5. Did you change the plan because of something you found? What was it?
6. What did you discover only after you started? Where were you when you found it?
7. What did you do then?
8. Was there a moment you almost chose a different route? What tipped it?

## Missing information (8 minutes)

9. When a map does not mention stairs or a step, what do you assume?
10. Have you ever seen a note from another traveler about access? What made you believe it or ignore it?
11. How recent does that kind of note need to be?
12. If two notes disagree, what do you do?

## Different days (6 minutes)

13. Does the constraint change from trip to trip? Tell me about two trips where it differed.
14. If you were planning for someone else, what would you need to ask them first?

## Tools (5 minutes)

15. Which apps or sites did you use on that trip? What did you trust them to do?
16. Was there a setting you turned on? What was it called, and did the result change in a way you could explain?

Do not ask "would you use a feature that…" 

## Concept, optional (6 minutes)

Only after the story is done. Show the route-comparison screen, or describe it in one sentence: three routes, one matching on confirmed data, one with unknowns, one with confirmed stairs.

17. In your own words, what is the difference between these three labels?
18. Which route would you open first, and why?
19. What is missing that you looked for on your real trip?

## Close (2 minutes)

20. What should I have asked about that trip and did not?
21. Thank them. Explain how notes will be stored.

## Moderator reminders

- Do not fill silence with the product's vocabulary. Let them name stairs, hills, and ramps first.
- If they ask what AccessPath is, say you will show a draft near the end.
- Write quotes with the participant code and question number. Do not tidy meaning in the quote itself.
