# Proto-personas

**Status:** Hypotheses. These are not based on interviews. Do not quote them as users. Names are invented. No photo is used, so the concept is not tied to a stock face.

Revise or delete each persona when research notes exist. If a persona's "need" never shows up in interviews, remove the matching preset only after checking the others.

## Maya Chen — step-free, and specific about evidence

**Hypothesis.** Uses a power wheelchair. Plans trips in a hilly neighborhood and has been sent down a "step-free" route that ended at a stair or a dead elevator.

**Goal:** Leave only after she can point at what is confirmed.
**Constraint she would set:** Avoid stairs, curb ramps on, grades under 8%.
**Frustration hypothesis:** A green badge that hides unknown blocks.
**Success:** She chooses Madison gentle and can say that Pike and Seneca is not a match because grade and a curb ramp are unknown.
**Not her whole life:** She is not a stand-in for every wheelchair user. Someone else may accept a steeper block.

## Andre Brooks — temporary, and not looking for a "disability app"

**Hypothesis.** Using a cane for several weeks after a knee injury. He will avoid stairs and does not want an app that names a diagnosis.

**Goal:** A calmer route for this month, then settings he can delete.
**Constraint he would set:** Avoid stairs, maybe no grade limit, or the gentle preset if hills hurt.
**Frustration hypothesis:** A profile called "disabled" or a setup he cannot turn off later.
**Success:** He saves nothing, uses settings for one trip, and deletes them if he did save.
**Design response:** Presets do not name a condition. Delete is on the saved screen.

## Priya Shah — different constraints on different days

**Hypothesis.** Sometimes walks with a stroller, sometimes with her father, who uses a walker. She needs today's settings, not a permanent identity.

**Goal:** Switch the preset without relearning the app.
**Constraint she would set:** Stroller or luggage (stairs off, curb ramps on, slopes under 12%) on one day; step-free or gentle on another.
**Frustration hypothesis:** One household profile that is wrong for half the trips.
**Success:** She can see that the stroller preset allows a grade the step-free preset rejects, and she can still edit it.

## Who is intentionally not a persona yet

Transit dispatchers, city asset managers, and professional access auditors. They matter to data partnerships. They are not the MVP traveler. Adding them before interviews would widen the product into a civic dashboard.
