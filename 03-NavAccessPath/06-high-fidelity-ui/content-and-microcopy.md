# Content and microcopy

## Voice

Plain, specific, and short. Name the street, the grade, the source, and the date. Do not praise the person. Do not apologize for the constraint. Do not say a route is safe, official, or fully accessible.

## Words to keep stable

| Use | Do not swap in |
| --- | --- |
| Confirmed | Verified, when talking about a surveyed attribute. Verified is reserved for a checked report or notice. |
| Community-reported | Crowdsourced, user-generated |
| Unknown | N/A, not available, blank |
| Pending review | Submitted, thanks, live |
| Matches your preferences on confirmed data | Accessible route, fully accessible, good to go |
| Conflicts with your preferences | Inaccessible, blocked for you |
| Some details are unknown | Might be fine |
| Avoid stairs | Wheelchair mode |
| Step-free | Disabled mode |

## Key sentences

- "Presets are starting points. They do not describe a person, and every setting stays editable."
- "Unknown means we have no data. AccessPath does not treat a missing detail as step-free, level, or open."
- "A match is not a guarantee that the path is barrier-free."
- "A community report is not a confirmation."
- "No route matches your preferences on confirmed data."
- "This report is not verified. Other people will not see it as a confirmed access detail."
- "AccessPath does not draw a route when it has no sidewalk data."
- "This prototype does not read or upload your device location."

## Buttons

| Label | Why |
| --- | --- |
| Save preferences | Writes this browser. |
| Use for this trip only | Does not write. |
| Compare routes | The job verb. |
| Mark on the map | Highlights. Does not choose. |
| View details | Opens segments. |
| Choose this route | Saves a recent trip. Does not navigate. |
| Report a barrier | Starts the form. |
| Submit report | Stores pending. |
| Withdraw report | Undo. |
| Not now | Declines location. |
| Try again | Repeats the failed comparison. |

## Errors

- "Choose two different places."
- "Set access preferences before comparing routes."
- "Choose a report category."
- "Choose where you found the barrier."
- "Describe what you found in at least a short sentence."
- "You're offline. This report was not submitted."

## Evidence reasons

Written by the evaluator so the card cannot drift from the rule. Patterns:

- "Stairs confirmed on Pine Street link."
- "Grade on Denny Way hill is about 10% confirmed, over your 8% limit."
- "Grade on Pike Street: unknown. Your limit is 8%."
- "A community report says there are no stairs on Community clear block. This is not confirmed."
- "Sidewalk surface on Western Avenue: unknown. Surface is not one of your preferences."

## Legal and safety tone

The product does not give official accessibility guidance. The sample banner is permanent in the prototype. A production version would replace it with the pilot city and the real source names, and would still refuse a guarantee.
