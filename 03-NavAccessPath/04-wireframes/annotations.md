# Wireframe annotations

Numbered marks on the boards match these notes. The PNGs are in `assets/wireframes/png/`.

## S01 Home

1. Empty preferences are a prompt, not a default wheelchair mode.
2. The trip starts only after the person can see that results will lead with preference fit.

## S02 Preferences

1. Step-free fills three settings. It is a starting point.
2. The filled settings stay visible so a preset is not a hidden profile.

## S03 Trip

1. The active preference set is repeated above the places.
2. Location is a separate step with a disclosure, not a silent read.

## S04 Results

1. The matching route is first even though it is the longest of the three.
2. The shortest route is still on the page, with the stair conflict in words.

## S05 Map

1. The legend defines solid, dashed, and marked lines in words.
2. The text list is not removed when the map is emphasized.

## S06 Detail

1. Segment facts are a definition list, not only a stroke on the drawing.
2. Choose and Report are separate. Choosing does not file a report, and reporting does not choose the route.

## S07 Entrance

1. Unknown stays on the step because no source is stored.
2. The step report and the ramp report are both pending. Neither deletes the other.

## S08 Report

1. Category and place are chosen, not free-typed, so the report can be tied to a segment.
2. The helper says the browser store is the only destination in this prototype.

## S09 Status

1. Pending review is the heading, not "thank you, verified."
2. Withdraw is available before any review exists.

## S10 Saved

1. Each setting is written as on or off, not only as a preset name.
2. Delete is scoped to this browser.

## S11 No match

1. The headline is the explanation. The bad route is not hidden.
2. Edit preferences is the recovery, not a silent relaxation of the grade limit.

## S12 Error

1. The failure names the sample service. It does not blame the person.
2. Try again repeats the same trip.

## S13 Offline

1. No route is guessed.
2. A report can be written and cannot be submitted.

## S14 Location

1. The dialog says the prototype will not read the device.
2. Not now is as strong as allow.

## S15 Loading

1. The status is a sentence. Reduced motion removes the wait, not the sentence.
