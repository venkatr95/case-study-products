# UVP and positioning

## Position

For someone deciding whether to spend today, MoneyMind is the 30-day cash estimate that shows the lowest point and the math behind safe to spend. It is for people willing to enter their own numbers. It is not a bank, a budget coach, or a net-worth dashboard.

## UVP

Know when you may run short of money before it happens.

The UVP is kept only if three things stay true in the interface:

1. The low point is visible, not just a single encouraging number.
2. The person can see which entered items created it.
3. The number is named as an estimate every time it is used to decide.

## What we will say

- “Estimate.”
- “Lowest point in the next 30 days.”
- “Set-aside you chose.”
- “Not a guarantee, a bank balance, or financial advice.”
- “Scenario, not saved.”

## What we will not say

- “You are safe.”
- “We predict your balance.”
- “You overspent.”
- “Recommended cushion.”
- “Your bank says.”

## Category

Personal cash-timing tool. Adjacent categories, and why MoneyMind does not join them yet:

| Category | Their promise | MoneyMind’s refusal |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Aggregator / tracker | All accounts in one place | No bank connection in the MVP |
| Envelope budget | Give today’s dollars jobs | Does not ignore future bills that post before payday |
| Month-left-to-spend | What remains in this calendar month | A shortfall can happen mid-month, before the month “runs out” |
| Long-range forecast | Retirement or net worth over years | The job is the next 30 days |
| Coaching or product offers | Advice, debt plans, card offers | Non-goal in the PRD |

## Strategic emphasis

Lead with the low point. The safe-to-spend figure is the headline because it matches the purchase decision, but it is always one tap from the items and the formula. A headline without the trough would collapse into the same “one magic number” pattern MoneyMind is trying to improve on.

## Offer for a first session

A person can get a useful estimate without linking a bank: balance, next income, bills before that income. Sample data exists so the story can be seen immediately, and it is labeled as not their finances. The activation hypothesis (three items in a first session) is a research target, not a dark-pattern quota. The empty state invites the next payday and the bills before it. It does not block the estimate.

## Brand voice in one line

Calm ledger. Specific, short, and unwilling to flatter or scold.
