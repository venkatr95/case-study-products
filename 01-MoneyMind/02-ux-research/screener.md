# Screener

Use this before inviting someone to the behavior interview or, later, the usability test. It is a filter, not a research finding.

## Introduction

“This is a 45-minute conversation about how you handle upcoming bills and spending decisions. It is a design study, not a bank and not a sales call. You would not link an account or share passwords. Does that sound worth a few questions to see if it’s a fit?”

## Questions

1. Are you 18 or older?
   - No → stop.
   - Yes → continue.
2. In the last month, did you personally decide how to spend your own money, not only as an accountant or adviser for someone else?
   - No → stop.
   - Yes → continue.
3. Which best describes your pay in the last three months? (They may choose more than one.)
   - Regular paycheck on a weekly, biweekly, or monthly cycle.
   - Hours or shifts that change the amount.
   - Freelance, contract, or other irregular deposits.
   - Something else: ____
   - I prefer not to say → continue, but do not force a persona.
4. In the last three months, have you had a bill or planned expense you were unsure you could cover?
   - Yes → continue.
   - No → continue only if other recruits already said yes. The study needs some recent episodes.
   - Not sure → ask: “Have you moved a purchase, paid something late, or checked your balance more than once before spending?” Yes → continue.
5. What do you use today to look at upcoming money? Choose any.
   - Bank or credit-union app.
   - Budget app. Which one, if you want to say: ____
   - Spreadsheet or notes.
   - I mostly remember.
   - Someone else handles it → if they never look themselves, stop for the primary segment.
6. Are you comfortable talking about a recent spending decision without showing account numbers?
   - No → stop.
   - Yes → invite.

## Quotas to aim for before changing the product

These are recruitment goals, not descriptions of real people already interviewed.

- 5–8 total.
- At least 3 with a regular pay cycle.
- At least 2 with irregular deposits.
- At least 2 who do not currently use YNAB, Monarch, Copilot, Simplifi, or PocketGuard, so the sample is not only expert budgeters.
- Do not recruit only coworkers who have seen this prototype.

## Usability-test add-on

For the later prototype test, add:

7. Can you join a 30-minute video call and share a screen, or meet in person?
8. Do you use a phone-sized browser or a desktop for personal admin? Either is acceptable. Note which, so the session uses their usual size.

## Do not score “good with money”

Rejecting people who feel disorganized would bias the study toward people who already have a system. The episode in question 4 is the qualifier.
