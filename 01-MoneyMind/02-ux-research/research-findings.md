## How the design should change after real findings

- If people already solve the timing problem with a calendar, the timeline has to be faster than that calendar or the concept is weak.
- If people refuse to type bills, sample data is only a demo. The MVP claim “useful without a bank” fails for them.
- If people describe month-end category guilt rather than a dated shortfall, the safe-to-spend formula is the wrong headline.
- If people hear any future number as a promise, labeling alone is not a sufficient guardrail. The usability test is where that is measured.

Until then, the prototype is built on the PRD hypothesis and the competitive gaps that were sourced, not on user evidence.
