# Jobs to be done

The PRD job is the only job treated as the design brief. The others are hypothesized related jobs. None are validated.

## Primary job (from the PRD)

When I am deciding whether I can afford a purchase, I want to understand my upcoming commitments and likely balance, so I can make an informed choice.

**Design translation:** Safe to spend answers “how much today.” The timeline and the explanation answer “why that number, and when it is lowest.” The what-if answers “what if the purchase is this amount on this date.”

**Done looks like:** The person can say the amount, the low-point date, one cause, and that it is an estimate. That is also the comprehension definition for the usability test.

**Not done:** They leave with a new budget personality, a savings goal, or a linked bank.

## Hypothesized related jobs

| Job | In this MVP | Why |
| --- | --- | --- |
| When a bill date moves, update the plan and see the new low point. | Yes. Edit, with a preview before save. | Same estimate, less rework. |
| When I might forget a low point, leave myself an alert. | Yes, in app. Device alerts only while the page is open. | FR-06, with the limitation stated. |
| When I want the numbers out of this browser, take them with me or erase them. | Yes. Export and delete. | Privacy requirement. |
| When I want to know if I stayed inside grocery categories last month. | No. The summary only totals what was entered for the next 30 days. | Different job. Competitors already do history. |
| When we share an account, show both people’s spending. | No. | Could-have, later. |
| When I want advice on what to cut. | No. | Non-goal. Advice language is a safety risk. |

## Forces, as hypotheses

**Push:** A balance that looked fine and then a bill hit. This has not been quoted from a participant.

**Pull:** A number that shows the dip before the purchase, with the bill named.

**Habit:** The bank app is already installed and trusted. MoneyMind has to be worth typing.

**Anxiety:** A wrong forecast could cause a worse decision than no forecast. The product answers with labels, not with confidence.

## Job story used in the prototype flow

When I open the app before a purchase, show me the lowest estimated point, let me try the purchase without saving it, and let me keep or discard that change on purpose.
