# Interview guide

45 minutes. The guide asks about a recent episode. It does not mention safe to spend, forecasts, or MoneyMind until the closing optional question, and that question is skipped if time is short.

## Before you start

Read the consent points in `research-plan.md` aloud. Confirm they are 18 or older. Ask permission to take notes. Start recording only after a yes.

“I’m learning how people decide whether they can spend money in the next couple of weeks. There are no right answers. You can skip any question. Please don’t show me account numbers or passwords.”

## 1. Context (5 minutes)

- How do you usually get paid, in your own words?
- Who else, if anyone, spends from the same money?
- What do you open when you want to know how much money you have? A bank app, a spreadsheet, paper, something else?

## 2. A recent episode (15 minutes)

- Think of the last time you weren’t sure whether you could afford a purchase or a bill. When was that?
- What was the purchase or the bill?
- What did you look at before you decided?
- What did that screen or note tell you, and what did it leave out?
- What did you do next?
- Did anything about the timing of your pay or your bills matter in that decision?
- How did it turn out? What, if anything, surprised you later?

If they cannot recall one episode, ask about the last bill that felt tight. Do not supply an example that points at a 30-day forecast.

## 3. Current system (10 minutes)

- Walk me through how you remember upcoming bills. What did you do the last time a bill was coming up?
- How do you handle a bill that is bigger than one paycheck, if that happens?
- Have you tried a budget app or a spreadsheet in the last year? What did you keep using, and what did you stop?
- What do you have to type, download, or fix to keep that system true?
- When the numbers are wrong or old, how do you notice?

## 4. Trust (8 minutes)

- Have you ever seen a money app or spreadsheet tell you that you had more available than you really did? What did it show?
- What would you need to see alongside a future balance before you relied on it?
- What would make you ignore it?

Do not ask “would you like an explanation?” as a yes/no. Ask what they would check.

## 5. Close (5 minutes)

- What should I have asked about how you handle the next few weeks of bills?
- Optional, only if the episode is finished: some concepts show an estimate of upcoming cash. What would you worry that estimate would get wrong?

Thank them. Do not demo the prototype in this interview.

## Moderator notes

- Stay with the last real episode if they drift into general opinions.
- Do not correct their method.
- If they mention overdrafts or hardship, do not probe for details they did not offer. Offer to skip ahead.
- Write their words. Do not tidy them into the product’s vocabulary during the session.
