# Content and microcopy

## Voice

Second person. Short sentences. Name the object (the estimate, the bill, the date). Do not praise or scold. Do not say “oops,” “uh oh,” “great job,” “you’re safe,” or “warning” as a moral label. “Alert” is allowed only as the name of the low-balance feature.

## Terms

| Term | Use for | Do not substitute |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Estimate | Any projected balance or safe-to-spend figure | Forecast, when it can be heard as a promise. Timeline is the screen name. |
| Safe to spend | The decision number | Available balance, leftover, free to spend |
| Set-aside | The cushion the person chose | Emergency fund, recommended buffer |
| Lowest estimate | The trough in the 30 days | Danger day, worst case |
| Plan | Saved items | Transactions |
| Scenario | The unsaved what-if | Draft budget |
| Sample data | The built-in illustrative plan | Demo mode, as a joke |

## Required lines

These appear in the prototype and should stay intact:

- “Estimates, not promises.”
- “Nothing connects to a bank.”
- “The result is an estimate. It is not a guarantee or financial advice.”
- “This portfolio prototype stores entries in this browser without encryption. Use approximate amounts. Do not enter bank passwords or full account numbers.”
- “Your choice, not a recommendation.”
- “Estimate only. Not a guarantee, a bank balance, or financial advice.”
- “Sample data. These figures are not your finances.”
- “Scenario · not saved.”
- “This prototype does not schedule background alerts.”

## Patterns

**Confirmation:** “Rent saved. The estimate includes it.” Name the item and say the estimate changed.

**Shortfall:** “No spend keeps the lowest point at or above the $100.00 set-aside. The estimate is short by $60.00 on Fri, Oct 30.” State both the zero people might spend and the gap.

**Empty:** “Add the next payday and the bills due before it. Until then, safe to spend is the starting balance minus any set-aside.”

**Stale:** “Balance date is Wed, Jan 1, 2020, N days ago. Spending since then is not in this estimate. Update the balance before you rely on it.” Use the real date and count.

**Validation:** Say what to enter. “Add a short name, such as Rent or Payday.” “Use dollars and up to two cents, such as 25.00.” “Choose today or a past date. The estimate starts from cash you already have.”

**Destructive confirm:** “Delete everything stored in this browser?” Body lists balance, items, alert, and snapshot. Confirm button: “Delete all data.” Never “Yes.”

**Permission denied:** “Notifications are blocked. MoneyMind will keep the alert on Home and on this screen. You can allow notifications in the browser’s site settings.”

## Numbers

Use the locale currency format, with cents. A minus sign for expenses is the typographic minus, and the word Expense is also on the screen. Do not rely on red.

## Sample story wording

When sample data is loaded, do not narrate it as “you are $90 safe” in the case study without saying the figures are illustrative. Inside the product, the banner carries that label so the sentences can stay concrete.
