# Visual direction

**Name:** Clear ledger.

MoneyMind should feel like a paper ledger that has been set in readable type, not like a trading terminal and not like a game. The audience may be anxious when they open it. Decoration that raises arousal works against the task. Trust here is legibility, stable numbers, and enough quiet to read a sentence.

## What it is not

- Neon green for “you’re up” and red for “you’re bad.” Income is not a win and a bill is not a moral event.
- A dark glass dashboard. One light theme is specified so contrast can be checked. A dark theme is not claimed.
- Illustration of people celebrating or worrying. The content is the numbers and the items.

## Signature elements

- Warm paper background `#F3EFE6` so it is not a generic white admin tool.
- A serif figure for safe to spend (Fraunces, with Georgia as the fallback) so the decision number is distinct from body text.
- Atkinson Hyperlegible for UI text when the font loads, otherwise Segoe UI. The face was chosen for reading, not for fashion.
- A teal `#0E4F4C` used for actions and for a non-shortfall figure. It is not “profit green.”
- A rust `#7A2E2A` only when the estimate is short of the set-aside, always with words.
- Hairline rules `#D5CBBA` instead of heavy cards and drop shadows.
- An “Estimate” pill that never leaves the header.

## Layout character

Wide margins, a single reading column on a phone, and a two-column home only when the window can hold the hero and the next bills side by side. Numbers use tabular figures so amounts line up.

## Sample and scenario

Sample uses an amber band `#F3E6C8` with amber text `#6A4B16`. Scenario rows use a dashed treatment and the words “Not saved,” not a second brand color that could be confused with income.

## Motion

Almost none. Focus rings and the absence of essential animation mean `prefers-reduced-motion` has little to disable. The stylesheet turns off remaining transitions anyway.

## Tone in the visuals

The screenshots in `assets/screenshots/` are the high-fidelity expression of this direction. The SVGs in `assets/wireframes/` are the structure without the color.
