# Portfolio summary

## Card

**MoneyMind** — Know when you may run short of money before it happens.

A 30-day cash estimate and an explainable safe-to-spend figure, built from amounts a person types. No bank connection. Prototype, wireframes, and research plan. Usability testing not yet run.

**Role:** Product design and prototype. **Year:** 2026 concept.

## Captions

1. **Home.** Safe to spend is the lowest estimated point minus a set-aside. The sample banner is part of the design: these figures are illustrative.
2. **Explanation.** Every row ties to a starting balance, an entered item, or the cushion the person chose. Later income is listed and excluded.
3. **Timeline.** The lowest day is named in text. A table repeats the chart so the dip is not color-only.
4. **What-if wireframe.** The scenario is dashed and labeled not saved. Adding it to the plan is a separate action.
5. **Onboarding wireframe.** The product states that it does not connect to a bank before it asks for a balance.
6. **Desktop home.** The decision and the next items sit side by side from 880px. The estimate pill stays in the header.
7. **Unreadable data.** A broken save is not deleted until the person confirms. They can download the raw text first.

## Annotation to keep on the hero

“Estimate only. Not a guarantee, a bank balance, or financial advice.”

## Do not claim on the portfolio page

- User quotes.
- A comprehension percentage.
- A comparison that says competitors have no forecasting.
- A Figma link.
- Production security.
