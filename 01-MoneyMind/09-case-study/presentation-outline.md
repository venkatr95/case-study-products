# Presentation outline

Eight slides. About eight minutes. Do not add a results slide.

## 1. The decision

**Title:** Know when you may run short, before it happens.

**On the slide:** Product name, the one-line UVP, “portfolio concept, validation planned.”

**Say:** This is a cash-timing tool. It is not a bank and not a finished study.

**Visual:** Home screenshot with the sample banner visible.

## 2. The balance is not the plan

**Title:** A balance hides the next bill.

**On the slide:** One sentence of the problem hypothesis. Label it as a hypothesis.

**Say:** The PRD’s bet is that people spend from a number that is already partly spoken for. I have not interviewed anyone yet. Here is how I would check.

**Visual:** None, or the journey map marked “hypothesis.”

## 3. What already exists

**Title:** The leftover number is crowded. The dated dip is not.

**On the slide:** Three sourced lines: PocketGuard’s safe-to-spend promise, Simplifi’s planned-spend gap in projected cash flow, Copilot’s backward-looking cash flow and lack of recurring income.

**Say:** I am not claiming those products are missing forecasts. I am choosing a narrower job.

**Visual:** The comparison table, shortened to three rows.

## 4. The definition

**Title:** Safe to spend is the low point, minus a cushion you set.

**On the slide:** The six-row formula. Note same-day bills before income, and income after the dip excluded.

**Say:** If I cannot show this math, the big number is just another slogan.

**Visual:** Explanation screenshot or WF-05.

## 5. The flow

**Title:** Try the purchase without saving it.

**On the slide:** Welcome → Home → Explanation → What-if → Discard or add.

**Say:** The scenario column is the interaction. Typing is not saving.

**Visual:** What-if wireframe WF-08.

## 6. Trust is a set of refusals

**Title:** No guarantee, no advice, no bank password.

**On the slide:** Disclaimer, stale banner, unreadable-data actions, notification limit.

**Say:** The prototype stores data in the browser without encryption and says so. That is a limit, not a security story.

**Visual:** Onboarding screenshot.

## 7. What I would test

**Title:** Six tasks, no pretended scores.

**On the slide:** Interpret the number. Find the cause. Afford $150 without saving. Add a bill. Remove it. Say what happens tonight if the page is closed.

**Say:** The PRD’s 70% and 10% figures are targets. With five people I would report counts, not a pass.

**Visual:** None.

## 8. What I would do next

**Title:** Interviews first. Bank linking last.

**On the slide:** Three next steps from the case study.

**Say:** If the interviews are about categories and guilt, this formula is the wrong product. I would rather learn that than polish the teal.

**Visual:** None.
