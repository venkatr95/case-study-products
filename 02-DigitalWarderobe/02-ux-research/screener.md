# Screener — Rewear discovery interviews

**Purpose:** Find people who recently decided what to wear, or recently packed, and can talk about that hour. This is not a test of fashion interest.

**Channel:** A short form or a recruiter call. Reject on the hard criteria. Do not reject because someone’s style sounds “basic.” The product is for ordinary wardrobes.

## Intro copy

We’re interviewing people about how they choose clothes in the morning and how they pack for short trips. 45 minutes. You don’t need to like fashion, and you won’t be asked to show photos of yourself. The interview is paid even if you skip questions.

## Questions

1. How old are you?
   - Under 18 → **stop**
   - 18 or older → continue
2. In the last two weeks, have you chosen an outfit for yourself for work, study, an event, or a day out?
   - Yes → continue
   - No → **stop** for the getting-dressed quota. They may still qualify for the trip quota via question 4.
3. In the last two weeks, was there at least one day when that choice took longer than you wanted, or you wore something mainly because it was easy?
   - Yes → getting-dressed quota
   - No → still eligible, mark as “low friction.” Do not fill the whole sample with low friction. Cap at 1 of 6.
4. In the last six months, did you pack clothes for a trip of at least two nights?
   - Yes → trip quota
   - No → fine for the getting-dressed quota
5. Do you already use a wardrobe app at least weekly (for example Whering, Stylebook, Acloset, Indyx, Cladwell, or similar)?
   - Yes → eligible, cap at 2 of 6 so the sample is not only power users
   - No → eligible
6. Are you comfortable talking about ordinary clothing decisions for 45 minutes?
   - Yes → eligible
   - No → **stop**
7. Anything you want us to know about access? For example, you use a screen reader, you prefer captions, or you need a break.
   - Free text. This is for logistics, not exclusion.

## Quotas for a round of 6

| Quota | Target | Why |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Getting-dressed story in the last two weeks | At least 4 | Primary job |
| Trip of 2+ nights in the last six months | At least 2 | Secondary job. One person can fill both quotas. |
| Weekly wardrobe-app users | At most 2 | Avoid a sample that only requests features |
| Low-friction answer on question 3 | At most 1 | Need the annoying mornings |

No quota on gender, body size, or budget. Record gender only if the participant offers it and it is relevant to a dress-code story. It is not an analysis axis by default.

## What the screener must not ask

- Body measurements, weight, or “what is your style?”
- Income or the brands they wear
- A photo
- “Would you download an app that suggests outfits?” That question recruits polite agreement.
