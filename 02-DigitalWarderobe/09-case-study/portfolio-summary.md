# Portfolio summary — Rewear

**Card title:** Rewear  
**One line:** A private wardrobe that turns clothes you already own into an explained outfit and a packing list.  
**Role:** Product design and prototype.  
**Status:** Concept. Validation planned. No participant metrics.

## Short paragraph

Rewear helps someone see combinations in their own closet and pack a trip without listing the same shoes twice. The case study is a full design trail and a working web prototype. It deliberately skips the social feed, the shop, and the body analysis that other wardrobe apps use. Suggestions show the tags they used. Nothing here has been usability-tested yet.
