# Test scenarios — Rewear

Read the task. Then stop talking. Success is observed, not declared by the participant’s politeness.

## Task 1 — Add an item without a photo

**Setup:** Fresh prototype, welcome screen.

**Read:** “Add one item of clothing to the wardrobe. Do not add a photo. Use any name and details you want. Tell me when you think it’s saved.”

**Success:** An item detail screen shows the name they typed, and the wardrobe contains it. Photo is empty.

**PRD link:** FR-01, guardrail, the “add five” metric’s first step.

**Watch:** Do they hunt for a skip-photo mode? Do they think Save is blocked? Time this. If it is already over three minutes, the first-outfit target is in trouble before an outfit exists.

## Task 2 — Find a navy item

**Setup:** Moderator loads the sample wardrobe and says the clothes are examples.

**Read:** “Find an item that is navy. You can search or use filters. Open it.”

**Success:** They open Navy merino knit or Navy overcoat.

**PRD link:** FR-03.  The 80% “find or generate” target includes this kind of find, but the official target is an outfit. This task is the filter check.

**Watch:** Can they clear a filter if they over-narrow?

## Task 3 — Save an outfit for work in cold weather

**Setup:** Sample wardrobe, outfits empty.

**Read:** “Save an outfit you could wear to work on a cold day, using these example clothes. The occasion should be work and the weather should be cold. Tell me when it’s saved.”

**Success:** Outfit detail shows Work and Cold, and at least two items, or a dress. They can say the occasion when asked “what kind of day is this outfit for?”

**PRD link:** FR-04, FR-05, time-to-outfit if you include task 1. On the sample, time this task separately so setup is not hidden inside it.

## Task 4 — Read a suggestion and dismiss one

**Setup:** Suggestions, set to Work and Cold if they are not already.

**Read:** “Look at a suggested outfit. Tell me, in your own words, why you think it was suggested. Then dismiss it, and tell me when that suggestion is gone.”

**Success:** Their explanation mentions at least one real factor on the card (work, cold, a layer, a color, or a wear count). The card they dismissed is no longer listed. They do not need to use the word “tag.”

**PRD link:** FR-07 comprehension, not just the click.

**Watch:** If they only describe the clothes and cannot point to a reason, the factor list is failing even if they tap Dismiss.

## Task 5 — Pack two outfits and spot the shared item

**Setup:** At least two saved outfits that share Brown loafers. If task 3 saved one, the moderator may save a second dinner outfit that also uses the loafers, and must say they did that.

**Read:** “Make a packing list for a trip of 4 nights that includes both of these outfits. Then tell me how many times the brown loafers appear on the list.”

**Success:** The list shows Brown loafers once, and the row mentions both outfits. Nights show as 4. They are not required to check every box.

**PRD link:** FR-06.

## Debrief

1. “What, if anything, felt like work you would not do at home?”
2. “Was there a moment that felt like the product was judging you? What did it say?”
3. “What would you want next that this did not do?” Write it down. Do not promise it.

## Early-stop rule

If task 1 fails for 3 people in a row, stop the later tasks for those sessions and fix the form. Continuing to outfits on a product they could not enter wastes the session. The moderator may still load the sample for a separate outfit observation, labeled as such.
