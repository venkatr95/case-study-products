# Design principles — Rewear

1. **The outfit is the product, the grid is the memory.** A beautiful closet that never becomes a saved outfit has not delivered the UVP. Every primary path ends in an outfit or a packing list made of outfits.

2. **Say what the rule was.** Suggestions, deletes, filters, and costs explain themselves in the same screen. If a rule cannot be written in a sentence, it does not ship in this MVP.

3. **Typing is not a degraded photo.** The add form is complete with no image. Failure of a photo leaves Save where it was.

4. **No body, no shop, no lecture.** Illustrations are garments. There is no face, no fit score, no “you might also like,” and no impact badge.

5. **Private by structure.** The prototype does not ask for an account, a location, or a contact. Weather is a preference. A trip note is a label.

6. **Controls stay dull and large.** 44px targets, named buttons, text under images. Warmth comes from type and paper color, not from decoration on the controls.

7. **Sample data cannot pretend.** If the clothes are illustrative, the interface says so on the wardrobe and on the item.
