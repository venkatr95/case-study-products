# Test scenarios

**Status:** Tasks for a future session. Not yet administered.
**Date:** 22 October 2026

Five tasks. U1 through U4 are the core. U5 checks trust and recovery. A smaller set would drop U5 before dropping U3.

Give the prototype at welcome with empty storage. Do not preload Maya unless U1 fails because they refuse to enter any skill. If you use Maya, say she is fictional and do not score that task as their own comprehension of their career.

## U1 — Choose a role and see where the profile comes from

**Say:** "Pick a role you would actually consider, or describe one that is not listed. Find out where the list of skills comes from."

**Done when:** They can point to the source sentence and the date, in their own words.

**Hint, only if stuck:** "Is there a place that says who wrote this?"

**Maps to:** FR-01. M3 setup.

## U2 — Add skills without a resume

**Say:** "Add two or three skills you have used at work or in a project. Include how sure you are. Do not paste a resume unless you want to later."

**Done when:** At least two skills are on the list with a proficiency and a confidence.

**Maps to:** FR-02. Release check that a resume is optional.

## U3 — Read the comparison

**Say:** "Using this page, name three skills this profile treats as regular work that you have not marked as both comfortable and sure. Tell me what the page is claiming about you."

**Done when:** The three skills match rows that are partial or not in their list, and they do not invent a percentage or a hiring chance. If they have fewer than three such rows, naming all of them is enough.

**Watch:** Whether an empty-to-full transition felt like a verdict. Whether extras felt like failures.

**Maps to:** FR-03. Metric M1 and M3. Design challenge.

## U4 — Save a plan and change it

**Say:** "Make a short plan from this comparison. Change the wording of one action so it sounds like something you would do. Mark one action done, then put it back."

**Done when:** At least one action is saved, one field was edited, Done was pressed, and Undo or Not started restored it.

**Watch:** Whether three feels like a cap they understand. Whether they can find undo.

**Maps to:** FR-04, FR-05. Metric M2.

## U5 — Data and the limit

**Say:** "Show me how you would remove everything this browser saved. Before you confirm, what would an employer learn from this site?"

**Done when:** They open the delete dialog and can say the product does not tell an employer their chances. They may cancel instead of confirming. Cancel is a success if they found the control.

**Maps to:** NFR deletion. Metric M3.

## Optional, if time

**U6 Paste.** "If you want, paste a job bullet you are comfortable sharing, or use the practice resume. Save only the suggestions you agree with."

**Done when:** An unchecked suggestion is absent from the skill list.

**Maps to:** FR-06.

Do not require U6. Pasting a real resume is optional and should be declined without comment.

## Retest criterion, for later findings

A fix is retested when a new participant in the same segment completes the same task with no moderator click, and their answer on the hiring question does not describe a score or a prediction.
