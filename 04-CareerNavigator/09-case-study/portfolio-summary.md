# Portfolio summary

**SkillBridge** helps a career changer compare skills they confirm with one role profile and leave with a few actions. The one-line promise is: turn the skills you already have into a clear path to your next role.

This is a portfolio concept with a working TypeScript prototype, annotated wireframes, and a written case study. It is not a validated product. It does not predict hiring. Interviews and usability tests are planned and have not been run.

**Role shown:** product design decisions, content, and the prototype.

**Best proof:** the comparison has no score, the empty state is not a gap wall, and the first plan is three editable actions.

## One paragraph for a card

SkillBridge is a career-navigator concept for people exploring a next role. I designed a comparison that uses matched, partial, and "not in your list" instead of a match rate, and a first plan capped at three actions. The prototype is a local TypeScript app with tests around the match rule and the sample journey. Public alternatives were reviewed on 22 October 2026. User interviews and usability tests are planned and have not been run, so the case study does not claim an outcome.
