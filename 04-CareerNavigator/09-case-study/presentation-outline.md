# Presentation outline

**Length:** 8 slides. Proposed talk, not a delivered one.
**Date:** 22 October 2026

Say "hypothesis" and "not tested with users" out loud on slides 2 and 7. Do not leave the room with the impression that Maya is a customer.

## 1. Title

SkillBridge. UVP: Turn the skills you already have into a clear path to your next role.

Portfolio concept. 22 October 2026. Validation planned.

Visual: `assets/screenshots/01-welcome-desktop.png`.

## 2. The problem, as a hypothesis

People translating current work into a new title meet a match rate or a long curriculum. The PRD bets that a transparent comparison and a small plan would help. That bet is untested.

Visual: none, or the journey diagram only if it is labeled hypothesis.

## 3. What nearby products optimize

One sentence each: Jobscan and Teal summarize a resume against a posting as a rate or score. roadmap.sh is a developer curriculum. CareerOneStop and O*NET describe occupations. Sources dated 22 October 2026.

Visual: no fake logos required. A table from the competitive analysis is enough.

## 4. The decision

Start from confirmed skills. Use matched, partial, and not in your list. Cap the first plan at three. Show the profile's source and date. Do not predict hiring.

Visual: `assets/diagrams/primary-flow.svg`.

Close on the welcome sentence: guidance, not a hiring prediction.
