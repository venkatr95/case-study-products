# Screen inventory

**Frame:** Phone column 390 × 844 inside a 1120-wide grayscale canvas. Annotations sit to the right of the phone.
**Responsive note on every frame:** One column under 720px. Primary navigation wraps. Targets are 44px. No match-score graphic.
**Date:** 22 October 2026

Visual files are SVG, generated by `assets/wireframes/build-wireframes.mjs`. They are wireframes, not the styled UI. The styled UI is the TypeScript app.
