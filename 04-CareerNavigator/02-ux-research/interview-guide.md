# Interview guide

**Status:** Guide only. It has not been used.
**Length:** 45 minutes
**Date:** 22 October 2026

## Before the session

Confirm they meet the screener. Read the consent points in `research-plan.md`. Do not show SkillBridge, the wireframes, or the PRD.

## Opening (5 minutes)

Thank them. Say the session is about how they have looked at a next role, not about testing them. Ask them to use a real recent example. If they would rather not name an employer, a description is enough.

## Recent behavior (25 minutes)

Use their last real search. Follow the order they describe. Prompts below are optional.

1. Think of the last time you considered a role that was different from what you do now. When was that, roughly?
2. What started it? A posting, a conversation, a course, something else?
3. Walk me through the first hour. What did you open, and what did you write down?
4. Which parts of your current work did you think might transfer? How did you decide?
5. Was there a skill you do at work that you were unsure how to name?
6. Did you compare yourself with a list of requirements? What was that list, and where did it come from?
7. What did you do next: apply, take a course, ask someone, stop, or something else?
8. Where did a list, a score, or a piece of advice feel too big or beside the point?
9. Did you keep any of that work? Where does it live now?
10. What would have made the next step obvious, using the tools you already had?

If they mention a match percentage or an ATS score, ask what they did because of the number. Do not suggest that the number was wrong.

## Language (8 minutes)

11. How would you describe your level at one skill you use often, to a friend?
12. What is the difference, for you, between "I have done this" and "I am sure I could do it in a new job"?
13. If a tool said a skill was "not in your list," what would you think it was claiming?

## Close (7 minutes)

14. Is there anything about that search I should have asked and did not?
15. Who else was involved (a friend, a manager, a recruiter)? Only if they want to say.
16. Thank them. Do not pitch the product.

## What not to ask

- "Would you use SkillBridge?"
- "Don't you hate match scores?"
- "How overwhelmed do you feel by learning plans?"
- Any question that needs them to imagine a hiring outcome.
