# Screener

**Status:** Draft. Not fielded.
**Date:** 22 October 2026

Use this before scheduling. One person can qualify through either path A or path B. Stop when six people are booked, with at least two in each path if both exist. Do not keep screening in order to force a mix that is not there.

## Introduction

We are talking with people who have recently looked at a different kind of work. The conversation is 45 minutes. It is not a job interview and it is not a skills test. You can decline any question.

## Questions

1. Are you 18 or older?  
   **No → stop.** Yes → continue.

2. In your work, do you regularly decide which candidates a company should interview or hire?  
   **Yes, that is a main part of my job → stop.** No → continue.

3. In the past six months, have you seriously considered a role that is different from your current or most recent job?  
   **Yes → path A.** No → question 4.

4. Are you in about the first three years of your career, and have you compared your experience with a job posting in the past six months?  
   **Yes → path B.** No → stop. Thank them.

5. Path A only. What kind of work are you in now, and what kind of role did you look at? One sentence each is enough.  
   Record the words. Do not score the answer.

6. Path B only. What kind of role did you compare yourself with?  
   Record the words.

7. Which of these did you actually use in that search? Choose all that apply.  
   - A job posting  
   - A resume or a rewrite of one  
   - A course list or a roadmap  
   - A spreadsheet or notes  
   - A conversation with someone who does the work  
   - A career site that describes occupations  
   - None of these  
   The question balances the sample. It does not include or exclude someone by itself.

8. Are you able to join a video call in English for 45 minutes?  
   **No → stop for this round.** The guide is written in English. A later round can add a language.

9. Is there any reason a recording, even with the option to skip questions, would be a problem?  
   If yes, offer a notes-only session. If they decline both, stop.

## What the screener does not ask

Age beyond 18, gender, race, disability, salary, or employer name. Those are not needed to learn the behavior in the guide.
